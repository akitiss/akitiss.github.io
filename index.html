About Me

Hi, I'm April. I'm a senior studying Mechanical Engineering at Columbia University with a strong background in robotics, rapid prototyping, and hands-on system design.

I'm especially interested in pursuing robotics, medical devices, and any space where clever mechanical design meets real-world impact. I love taking ideas and turning them into working prototypes, building mechanisms that solve real problems, and improving the way people interact with technology.

My goal is to lead development on meaningful engineering projects and contribute to solutions that genuinely make a difference.
