--- layout: project title: "NASA Micro-G NExT National Finalist: AST-ROID Device" goal: Create a device that allows a suited astronaut to collect a contact sample of regolith on the lunar surface and capture the grain orientation of the surface particles. images: - /static/img/nasa1_1.png - /static/img/nasa1_2.png ---

The device works similarly to a standard self-inking stamp. The carriage inside the stamp contains an adhesive where it faces away from the ground. As the astronaut presses down on the handle, the device is actuated and the carriage flips around and the adhesive side faces down onto the floor.

The device was fully machined using the mechanical engineering lab at Columbia, utilizing the Tormach mill and lathe. Some components were also 3D printed and laser cut.
