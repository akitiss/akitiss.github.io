NASA Micro-G NExt: Tool Belt Connection

Goal: Develop a device that allows a suited astronaut to quickly and reliably secure a tool to their tool belt, while maintaining single‑fault tolerance.

Solution: The design uses a double‑latch mechanism. One end attaches to the belt using a guiding rod, and the other end connects to the tool so it can slide onto the rod easily, even without visual confirmation. he mechanism is shaped to guide the tool into place by touch alone, and the dual latches ensure that once engaged, the tool remains securely fastened.
