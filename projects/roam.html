--- layout: project title: "Robotic Manipulation and Mobility Laboratory: Edgeless Skin" tags: - SOLIDWORKS - Mechanical Design - Resin Printing - Rapid Prototyping - Arduino - Circuit Wiring - Motors - 3D Printing problem: There is still yet to be a fully enclosed artificial 3D skin tissue that can be shaped into complex body parts, such as the hands. goal: Create a fully mechanical finger that the skin tissue can adhere to and be fully enclosed. images: - /static/img/roam_1.png - /static/img/roam_2.png ---

Created a structure similar to a bendy straw, the flexion part of the finger was created, allowing for 45 degrees of rotation. A bone was attached inside to allow for the rotation to be controlled by a motor. Holes are spaced throughout the finger to allow for nutrients to flow inside the finger and keep the skin alive.

The finger rests inside a boxed compartment to allow for the finger's 3D skin tissue to properly cure. Test printed the finger with different 3D print materials and resin.
