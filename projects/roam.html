Robotic Manipulation and Mobility Laboratory: Edgless Skin

Problem: There is still yet to be a fully enclosed 3D skin tissue that can be shaped into complex body parts, such as the hands.

Goal: Create a fully mechanical finger that the skin tisue can adhere to and be fully enclosed.

Goal: Created a structure similar to a bendy straw, the flexion part of the finger was created, allowing for 45 degrees of rotation. A bone was attached inside to allow for the rotation to be controlled by a motor. Holes are spaced throughout the finger to allow for nutrients to flow inside the finger and keep the skin alive. Finger rests inside a boxed compartment to allow for the finger 3D skin tissue to properly cure. Tst printed finger with different 3D print materials and resin
