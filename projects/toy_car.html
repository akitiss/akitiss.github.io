Toy Car

Goal: Replicate a 90s racing car using CAD, manufacture a scaled‑down version, and develop a manufacturing plan suitable for mass production.

Solution: Designed and manufactured the Maserati Tipo V4 using SOLIDWORKS, machining, silicone casting, and injection molding. The car body was produced using a two‑part mold and silicone casting. Wheels were fabricated through aluminum mold machining on the Tormach followed by injection molding. Additional components were manufactured using 3D prints and then integrated into the final assembly.
