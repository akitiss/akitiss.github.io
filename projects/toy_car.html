Designed and manufactured the Maserati Tipo V4 using SOLIDWORKS, machining, silicone casting, and injection molding.

  • Body: Produced using a two‑part mold and silicone casting.
  • Wheels: Fabricated through aluminum mold machining on the Tormach, followed by injection molding.
  • Additional components: Manufactured using 3D prints and integrated into the final assembly.
